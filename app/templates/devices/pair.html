What happens next?

  1. Identify. The QR code is read and checked on the server.
  2. Confirm. You see the device ID and type before registering anything.
  3. Register. The outlet is linked to your account and the code is consumed.
  4. Connect. Configure the SmartOutlet's Wi-Fi through its local setup page, then it will appear Online on your dashboard.

Registering does not put the outlet online. The SmartOutlet must connect to your router Wi-Fi and authenticate with the PowerAble server before it shows as Online.
